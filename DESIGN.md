## Brand & Style

This design system embodies modern pastoral elegance, spiritual reverence, and warm, open-armed community hospitality. Designed for an evangelistic ministry and production house, the aesthetic balances the timeless majesty of devotional ministry with the clarity and accessibility of contemporary digital media. 

The aesthetic philosophy draws on **Warm Editorial Contemporary**:
- Deep midnight architectural framing creates contemplative depth.
- Luminous gold elements evoke celestial light, sacred dedication, and premium craftsmanship.
- Organic, warm parchment foundations reject sterile digital whiteness in favor of an inviting, sanctuary-like reading experience.
- Generous editorial margins and balanced text proportions grant Scripture, sermon media, and testimonies room to breathe.
- Clear, welcoming community touchpoints (such as direct pastoral contact and live service streams) stand out through vivid, intentional contrast.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional progression from grounded contemplation to luminous inspiration.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#0d1b2a`, `#1a2744` - Midnight Navy):** The structural bedrock. Used for authoritative titles, dark hero backdrops, primary navigation, footers, and prominent text. Evokes solemnity, wisdom, and eternal stability.
- **Secondary (`#c59b27`, `#d4af37`, `#f3e5ab` - Radiant Gold Spectrum):** Used for spiritual highlights, badge flourishes, fine borders, active states, and decorative sacred accents. `#f3e5ab` serves as a soft, ethereal background wash for gold-accented callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#25d366` - Emerald Connectivity):** Dedicated exclusively to immediate pastoral care, prayer requests, WhatsApp ministry connections, and live real-time outreach channels.
- **Backgrounds & Neutrals:**
  - Base Page Surface: `#faf9f6` (Warm Alabaster / Parchment light)
  - Elevated Container / Card Surface: `#ffffff` (Pure Sanctuary White)
  - Inset / Well Surface: `#f4f1ea` (Soft Linen)
  - Body Text: `#1e293b` (Deep Slate Slate for softer contrast than pure black)
  - Muted Text: `#64748b` (Soft Charcoal for metadata and scripture citations)
  - Subtle Border: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)` or `#e7e3d8`

## Typography

The pairing blends the editorial gravitas of classic liturgical press with the crystalline readability of contemporary mobile-first interfaces.

- **Headlines & Scripture (`Playfair Display`):** Carries emotional depth, reverence, and oratorical weight. Used for page titles, message themes, and biblical citations. Italics are reserved for scripture quotes and personal pastoral blessings.
- **Interface & Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides a warm, open, geometric counterbalance. The generous x-height and rounded terminals eliminate visual fatigue during long sermon study notes and daily devotionals.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Rendered with elevated letter spacing and medium/semibold weights to maintain authority even at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict **8-point base rhythm** embedded within an editorial **12-column responsive fluid grid**.

### Form Factor Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column structure, `16px` margins, `16px` gutters. Primary CTAs lock into full-width tap targets.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column structure, `32px` margins, `24px` gutters. Content containers transition into dual-column reading flows.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column grid with a maximum content bound of `1280px`. Generous outer margins (`48px` minimum) create an uncluttered, serene reading frame.

### Spatial Principles
- **Sacred Breathing Space:** Sections never crowd each other; vertical rhythm between core sections uses `space-3xl` (72px) or `space-4xl` (96px) to signify thematic transitions (e.g., transition from Live Stream hero to Sermon Series grid).
- **Proximity:** Related scripture titles and verse references use compact spacing (`space-xs`), binding devotional content structurally.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy blends soft atmospheric elevation with warm tonal framing rather than cold, technical drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Shadow Palette
Shadows are tinted with the deep blue foundation rather than harsh neutral blacks:
- **Level 1 (Subtle / Cards):** `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.04)`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Media Cards):** `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.04)`
- **Level 3 (Modals / Sticky Navigation / Live Alerts):** `0 20px 35px -8px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.16), 0 1px 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`

### Surface Tiers & Overlays
- **Midnight Sanctuary Layer:** Dark containers (`#0d1b2a`) feature subtle radial light leaks from the center using translucent gold (`radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12), transparent 70%)`).
- **Gilded Borders:** Subtle hairline boundaries (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`) outline high-value surfaces to denote spiritual dignity without visual weight.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard. This decision preserves the formal, dignified structure expected of an established ministry, while softly rounded corners remove technological sterility and welcome personal interaction.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Form controls, inputs, scripture tag chips, and small operational badges.
- **Container Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Sermon cards, testimony modules, ministry production thumbnails, and event modals.
- **Elevated Focal Radius (0.75rem / 12px):** Hero feature banners and dedicated pastoral contact portals.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Reserved strictly for action-oriented CTAs (e.g., "Ver en Vivo", "Escríbenos por WhatsApp") to highlight them as friendly, highly interactive invitations.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Celestial Action:** Deep midnight background (`#0d1b2a`), radiant gold border (`1px solid #d4af37`), crisp white text with soft gold hover sheen (`#1a2744`). Fully rounded pill shape for inviting accessibility.
- **Radiant Gold Accent CTA:** High-contrast warm gold surface (`#c59b27`), dark text (`#0d1b2a`), bold weight. Reserved for tithes/offerings, event registrations, and live broadcasts.
- **WhatsApp Direct Ministry:** Dedicated emerald green (`#25d366`), pure white bold text, Level 2 elevation with soft emerald glow on hover. Accompanied by the recognizable messaging icon.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, midnight navy border (`#1a2744`) or gold hairline border on dark sections, shifting to subtle cream on hover.

### Cards & Devotional Modules
- **Sermon & Production Media Cards:** Built on crisp `#ffffff` over `#faf9f6` canvas. Features a 16:9 thumbnail frame with a delicate 1px inset gold border. Bottom content includes sermon title in `Playfair Display`, speaker badge, and scripture metadata.
- **Scripture Callout Cards:** Contained within `#f4f1ea` linen parchment, anchored by a deep midnight left border (4px) with subtle warm gold quote marks flanking the verse.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Small, uppercase (`label-sm`), pill-shaped badges. "En Vivo" (Live) features a pulsating ruby red indicator; sermon categories ("Fe", "Familia", "Alabanza") feature soft gold backgrounds (`#f3e5ab`) with midnight navy lettering.

### Inputs & Forms (Prayer Requests & Contact)
- **Input Fields:** Warm parchment interior (`#ffffff`), bordered by muted warm stone (`#dcd8cd`). On focus: smooth transition to a 1.5px radiant gold ring (`#c59b27`) without aggressive browser glow. 
- **Labels:** Semibold `Plus Jakarta Sans`, positioned cleanly above inputs with subtle guidance text below.

### Lists & Sermon Archives
- **Sermon Series Index:** Clean, horizontal stack separated by delicate gold-tinted dividing lines (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`). Highlights play duration, scripture reference, and instant download/listen actions on hover.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Rounded corners for checkboxes (3px radius), deep midnight fill with crisp white checkmark when active; resting state features a clean neutral slate border.
