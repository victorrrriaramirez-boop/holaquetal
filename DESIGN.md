## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a sanctuary of community, faith, and forward-looking spirituality. The tone balances deep reverence with warm, open-door hospitality. It rejects outdated church website clichés—such as literal parchment textures, harsh neon accents, or overbearing layouts—in favor of a contemporary editorial experience rooted in serene structure, breathing room, and intentional craft.

The visual direction follows a **Modern Editorial Minimalist** philosophy with warm ambient tactile elements:
- Generous whitespace and measured vertical rhythm evoke peace and contemplative reflection.
- Grounded midnight blues establish an authoritative and reliable foundation.
- Luminous golden amber accents bring warmth, divine celebration, and clear points of action.
- Soft neutral ivory grounds the experience in approachability, preventing the stark sterile detachment of pure digital white.
- The interface speaks with clarity, emotional warmth, and dignity across ministry branches, livestreams, sermons, and community events.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to invoke peace, clarity, and warmth.

### Primary Role (Midnight Navy)
- `#0F172A` acts as the primary anchor for dominant structural elements, primary buttons, hero sections, and high-contrast typography.
- `#1E293B` provides a softer secondary dark tier for navigation bars, footer canvases, and deep surface containers.

### Secondary Role (Golden Amber)
- `#D97706` serves as the primary accent, highlighting sacred moments, badge highlights, live broadcasts, active tab bars, and secondary call-to-actions.
- `#F59E0B` introduces a vibrant, radiant glow for hover states, prayer request indicators, and interactive micro-details.
- `#FEF3C7` is utilized for subtle, high-warmth amber tints behind tags and devotional highlights.

### Supporting & Neutral Canvas
- **Page Canvas:** `#FDFBF7` (Warm Alabaster/Ivory) provides an inviting paper-like backdrop that relieves eye strain and promotes warmth.
- **Card Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) creates crisp elevated cards floating above the ivory backdrop.
- **Borders & Dividers:** `#E2E8F0` with occasional shifts to `#F1F5F9` for ultra-subtle separations.
- **Text Tiers:**
  - Primary Text: `#0F172A` (95% contrast)
  - Secondary / Body Text: `#334155` (Balanced readability)
  - Muted / Meta Text: `#64748B` (Timestamps, scripture references, metadata)

## Typography

The pairing creates a deliberate balance between academic reverence and human accessibility:
- **Newsreader** delivers the literary gravity of scripture, sermon themes, and main editorial anchors. Its italic variants are especially suited for biblical excerpts, mission statements, and pastoral reflections.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a warm, humanist geometric base for high-frequency interactive content, navigational clarity, form inputs, and multimedia descriptions.

### Formatting Rules
- Hero titles and sermon topic displays must never be capitalized in full uppercase; always preserve natural title or sentence case to sustain warmth.
- Scripture verses should utilize the dedicated `scripture-quote` style, set in Newsreader italic with slightly elevated leading and indented borders.
- Metadata tags (sermon series, dates, speaker titles) utilize `label-sm` with slight uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.04em`) to establish crisp informational anchors.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial foundation adheres to an 8px base rhythm, favoring open layouts that cultivate a peaceful browsing state.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `16px` margins and `16px` gutters. Navigation compresses into a clean floating bottom dock or minimalist header drawer.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `24px` margins and `24px` gutters. Two-column card grids for sermon archives and upcoming events.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with max container width constrained to `1200px` for portal interfaces and `780px` for reading views (sermon transcriptions, devotionals, beliefs statements).

### Rhythmic Guidance
- Section gaps transition with expansive vertical spacing (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) to allow content to settle without visual overcrowding.
- Inner card padding follows a consistent `space-lg` (24px) for desktop and `space-md` (16px) for mobile.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate ambient layering rather than aggressive dropshadows, evoking natural daylight and warmth.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** The base ivory surface (`#FDFBF7`) without shadows.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface):** Pure white cards with an imperceptible warm tint shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03);`
  Paired with a hairline border: `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Media):** Sermon cards, event blocks, and interactive surfaces elevate softly on cursor approach:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 12px 32px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.05);`
  Transform translates subtly `-2px` on the Y-axis.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Nav):**
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12);`
  Surfaces employ a backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` fill.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry:
- Standard elements (cards, text fields, dialogue sheets) utilize `0.5rem` (8px) to `1rem` (16px) radius, conveying warmth, security, and approachability.
- Action items, filter pills, livestream badges, and audio scrubbers utilize full pill shapes (`9999px`) to emphasize touch-friendliness and dynamic interaction.
- Media elements (video thumbnails, worship galleries) feature gentle `12px` to `16px` corner curvature, softening photographic imagery into the page flow.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Call to Worship / Plan a Visit / Give):** Deep midnight background (`#0F172A`) with pure white text, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `12px 28px`. On hover: `#1E293B` with subtle scale transition.
- **Secondary Action (Watch Online / Learn More):** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#D97706` (Amber) with text in `#B45309`. On hover: subtle amber fill `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`.
- **Ghost Action:** Muted navy text (`#334155`) with minimal padding, underlaid by a golden underline transition on hover.

### Multimedia & Sermon Cards
- **Sermon Card Container:** Pure white background, `16px` border radius, subtle border.
- **Media Header:** 16:9 ratio image container with a dark vignette scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`). A floating amber play badge (`48px` circular pill with white icon) centers on hover.
- **Card Content:** Category label in amber uppercase (`label-sm`), sermon series title in `Newsreader` (`title-md`), speaker and date metadata in `body-sm` neutral.
- **Action Footers:** Integrated quick-action buttons for "Listen Audio", "Notes PDF", and "Share".

### Live Stream Status Badge
- Pill-shaped chip featuring a pulsating live dot (`#DC2626` or `#D97706`), text set in `label-sm` ("EN VIVO / SERVICIO DOMINICAL"), framed with a gentle luminous ring.

### Form Inputs & Prayer Requests
- **Input Fields:** Generous touch targets (`48px` minimum height), warm white background (`#FFFFFF`), `1px` stroke `#CBD5E1`.
- **Focus State:** Transitions to `#D97706` amber border with a soft `3px` ambient halo (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.15)`).
- **Text Areas:** Editorial-proportioned for prayer requests with subtle encouraging placeholder text.

### Scripture Verse Callouts
- Centered or asymmetric card with an intentional left accent bar (`3px` solid `#D97706`).
- Background: Soft ivory tint (`#F8FAFC`).
- Text: Newsreader 20px italic, followed by book, chapter, and verse attribution set in `label-md` tracking.

### Community & Ministry Group Chips
- Compact pill elements with `8px 16px` padding.
- Selected: `#0F172A` fill with white text.
- Unselected: White surface with `#E2E8F0` border and `#475569` text.
